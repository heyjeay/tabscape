Moon icons

The crescent from Tabscape's logo on its own, in iOS 26's Liquid Glass style. Each is shown large, and on a home screen at full and small size.

1Midnight glass

The logo's night sky and violet glow, with a warm, frosted moon.

2Clear

iOS's Clear look: a colourless glass moon on pale frost.

3Aurora

The start page's four section colours, blurred into a glow behind a white glass moon.

4Gold on black

Dark mode: a golden glass moon glowing on black, with a few stars.

5Tinted violet

iOS's Tinted look in Tabscape's violet: all one colour, glass on deep indigo.

6Layered

Two glass layers, as Icon Composer builds them: a pale full-moon disc behind the crescent.

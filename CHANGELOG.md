# What's new in Tabscape

## 1.3.0: Customize to your heart's content

The biggest update yet, and it brings in 1.2.0 too, which never went to the stores on its own. Everything new since 1.1.2:

### A new settings drawer
- **Customize** now slides in from the right, with everything sorted into categories down the side: Background, Layout, Fonts, Links, Bookmarks, Sync & backup, and Help & about.
- The page stays sharp behind it, with no blur or dimming, so you see every change as you make it.
- It reopens on the category you were last in, and Escape or a click on the page closes it.

### Fonts and sizes
- 14 fonts to choose from: Google Sans, your system font, Inter, Lexend, Outfit, Nunito, Comfortaa, Space Grotesk, Fraunces, Playfair, JetBrains Mono, Orbitron, Caveat and the pixel font Press Start.
- The greeting and date, section titles and links each get **their own font and their own size**.
- Every font is built in. Nothing is downloaded, and a new tab only loads the fonts it uses.

### Pages
- Spread your links over **more than one page**: add, rename and remove pages while editing, and choose which page each section sits on.
- Switch pages from the switcher at the bottom, with ← and →, with a sideways swipe on a trackpad, or **by scrolling up and down** (you can turn this off in Layout).
- Optionally, the page switcher can **slide away when you're not using it**, and come back when you point near the bottom.
- Pages slide in smoothly, and the greeting stays perfectly still while they do.

### Icons and hover effects
- **Site icons** next to every link: a colourful letter by default, the real site icon if you allow it, or **your own image**: click a link's icon while editing to pick one.
- **8 hover animations**: Fade, Lift, Expand, Glow, Slide, Glitch, Pixelate, or None. Point at each in the settings to preview it.

### Bookmarks sort themselves (optional)
- Turn on **Sort new bookmarks into sections** and anything you bookmark lands in the section it belongs in: Steam in Gaming, GitHub in Dev, and so on.
- A note shows where each one went, with **Move to** and **Undo**. Move one and Tabscape remembers where that site goes next time.
- Pick **+ New section…** in the note to make a section for it on the spot.
- If you bookmark something that's already on your page, the note tells you where it is.
- **Sort my last 20** catches up on bookmarks you made before turning it on.

### Moving links around
- **Move to** on every link while editing sends it to any section, on any page.

### Fixes
- **Sync now brings your links to a new computer.** Earlier versions could sync sections with no links in them; 1.3.0 saves them properly, and recovers what older versions synced.
- No more stray scrollbar, or header jumping, when switching pages.

### Privacy
- Bookmarks and real site icons each need a permission, and Tabscape only asks when you switch that feature on. Turning it off gives the permission back.
- As always: no accounts, no tracking, and nothing sent anywhere. Your images stay on your computer and are never synced.
